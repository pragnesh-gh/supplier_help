## Your mission

A train is made of tens of thousands of parts. Each must come from the right supplier: on time, in top quality, at low risk.

Rail procurement teams choose suppliers from hundreds of options. Build a tool that helps buyers explore supplier risk data, ask questions in plain language and compare vendors – turning data into decisions.

Imagine a buyer sourcing a critical component: they narrow the field down, compare up to 20 suppliers side by side and justify their final choice.

## The challenge

### 🖥️ Build a UI

Let buyers filter, rank and compare up to 20 suppliers at once. Go beyond tables: risk profiles, side-by-side views, ...

### 💬 Add a chatbot

Answer questions grounded in the data, e.g.

- "Which of SUPP_00012, SUPP_00450 and SUPP_00731 has the lowest financial risk?"
- "Why is the Total Risk Index of SUPP_00018 so high?"

### 🌐 Add a research agent

A buyer names a real company – your agent takes over: plans its research, crawls news, public sources and company sites, digs deeper when it finds red flags and delivers a risk report with source links.

### ✨ Surprise us

Go further with your own ideas, e.g. multi-agent workflows, dynamically generated UIs for chatbot answers, or auto-generated shortlist memos.
