## 8. Documentation

**How the scores work**

- Each risk area gets a score from 0 to 100; higher means more risk. Bands: Low 0–24 · Medium 25–49 · High 50–74 · Critical 75–100.
- Scores come from fixed rules that turn each verified fact into points, using published methods where they exist (credit ratings, the Altman Z-score, World Bank governance indicators). The total is a weighted average of the areas, with the weights of the challenge dataset.
- An area with no verified fact is “Not assessed”: it gets no score, never 0. With less than half of the score weight assessed, no total is shown.
- Two override rules apply: a current red flag from a trusted source puts its area at least at 50 (High), and a knock-out from a trusted source makes the total Critical.
- Flag colours are set by fixed rules, not by the AI. A fact found only on a site not in our trusted source list counts for less, is at most a watch item and is never a knock-out.
- Area weights: Operations & Delivery 30%, Financial 16%, People & Safety 15%, Legal & Governance 13%, Country & Location 13%, Reputation & Outside View 13%.
- Total in this report: weighted average of 11 of 18 sub-scores = 17.34 / 0.72; coverage 72 %; if unassessed scores were 0 or 100: 17-45.

**Heatmap rule**

Severity comes from the flag colour; likelihood from how well the fact is verified. Only red flags and watch items are placed. Rules are read top to bottom; the first that fits applies.

- Severity: Red flag → High
- Severity: Watch item, rated low severity → Low
- Severity: Watch item → Medium
- Likelihood: Found only on sites not in our trusted source list → Unconfirmed
- Likelihood: Claim alleged or claim unclear → Possible
- Likelihood: Confirmed by at least two independent sources, one of them trusted → Likely
- Likelihood: Stated by the authority or the company itself → Likely
- Likelihood: Any other case (one trusted source) → Possible
